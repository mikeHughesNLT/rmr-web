## OUTDOOR SPACES

**Hiking**
The owners control roughly 91 acres total: the 25-acre main property + an adjacent 20 acres + 46 acres on Red Mountain itself. There are trails throughout — from the lodge grounds up through old-growth forest on Red Mountain. It's real hiking, not a manicured path.

**The Gold Mine**
There's a historic early-1900s gold mine on the property. The entrance is Indiana Jones-level impressive — solid rock all the way in, about 40 feet into the hillside, with a natural freshwater spring inside. The owner's uncle claims to have found gold in it. That's... unverified. Guests discover it on the trails.

**Parking**
Plentiful. 6+ paved spots normally. For larger events and dry-season gatherings, additional parking opens up on the lawn. Generally not an issue.

**Outdoor gathering spaces**
- Expansive lawn for games, outdoor dining, gatherings
- Multiple decks and seating nooks — morning coffee, stargazing, afternoon reading
- Outdoor fire pit with firewood provided (Great Room + primary suite + fire pit)
  - Owners' land brings down more than enough trees each winter. Guests never run out of firewood.

## WEATHER & NATURE

- Mountain rainforest climate — prepare for all weather
- Summer: can be hot. Spring/Fall: heavy rains. Winter: snow and ice possible.
- Power outages possible in severe weather — no refunds per policy
- Bring provisions: nearest large grocery is Bellingham (~45 min). Small markets on Mt. Baker Highway for forgotten items.

**Wildlife**
Black bears are present in the area. These are black bears only — not brown bears, not grizzlies. They are not aggressive and have never historically been a nuisance or threat in this area. The main concern is trash/food left outside. As a precaution, bear spray is not a bad idea. Do not leave food or garbage outdoors.

Deer are plentiful and frequently spotted on the property — they blend beautifully into the surroundings. One of the real treats: deer often pass underneath the treehouse sauna deck.

Other wildlife on property: ants, bats, mice, raccoons, bobcats (we share the land with all of it).

**No hunting on the property.**

**Firearms:** For any questions about firearms, contact Mike directly via the contact page. (Mike appeared on History Channel's Top Shot, Season 3.)
