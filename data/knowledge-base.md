**The owners:** Mike and Angie Hughes. Mike grew up in this house. The house is now fully renovated and modernized — approximately 50 years old — but the bones are authentic: cedar milled from trees on the property, genuine log construction with modernized chinking and insulation. It's a rare thing: a classic log home that's also genuinely comfortable. Mike and Angie live adjacent to the property and are generally available if guests need anything — while still maintaining exceptional privacy. You have the space and the trees. They're nearby if you need them.

## OUTDOOR SPACES

**Hiking**
The owners control roughly 91 acres total: the 25-acre main property + an adjacent 20 acres + 46 acres on Red Mountain itself. There are trails throughout — from the lodge grounds up through old-growth forest on Red Mountain. It's real hiking, not a manicured path.

**The Gold Mine**
There's a historic early-1900s gold mine on the property. The entrance is Indiana Jones-level impressive — solid rock all the way in, about 40 feet into the hillside, with a natural freshwater spring inside. The owner's uncle claims to have found gold in it. That's... unverified. Guests discover it on the trails.

## LOCATION & NEARBY

| Destination | Distance |
|-------------|----------|
| Silver Lake Park | 5 min |
| North Fork Brewery (Glacier, WA) | 20 min |
| Canadian border — Sumas/Abbotsford crossing | 25 min |
| Cactus Club Café, Abbotsford BC | ~30 min |

**Silver Lake Park:** Kayaking, fishing, swimming, picnicking. Right in the neighborhood — 5 minutes away.

**North Fork Brewery (Glacier, WA — 20 min):** Legendary pizza. They craft their own beer. Great ambience. A genuine must-visit for anyone staying in the area — one of the best restaurants in rural Whatcom County. northforkbrewery.com

**Glacier, WA:** The last town before Mt. Baker. A general store, a handful of restaurants including North Fork Brewery, classic mountain village feel.

**Mt. Baker Ski Area:** Deep powder, legendary snowfall, uncrowded runs. Summer hiking and wildflower trails equally spectacular. 52 min from the property.

**Canada — Abbotsford, BC (~30 min with a clear border):**
Cross at the Sumas, WA / Abbotsford, BC border (about 25 min from the property). **Bring passports — everyone in your group.** Once across, Abbotsford has excellent restaurants, craft breweries, shopping, and international dining. Top pick: **Cactus Club Café** (34650 Delair Rd, Abbotsford) — a upscale Canadian casual chain, very well-regarded, great cocktails and food. Vancouver day trips are very doable for longer stays.
- Border wait times: bwt.cbp.gov
- Cactus Club Abbotsford: cactusclubcafe.com/locations/abbotsford/

**Bellingham (45 min):** Full services — Costco, grocery stores, Whatcom Falls Park, waterfront, breweries. Your main supply run destination.

## WEATHER & NATURE

- Mountain rainforest climate — prepare for all weather
- Summer: can be hot. Spring/Fall: heavy rains. Winter: snow and ice possible.
- Power outages possible in severe weather — no refunds per policy
- Bring provisions: nearest large grocery is Bellingham (~45 min). Small markets on Mt. Baker Highway for forgotten items.

**Wildlife**
Black bears are present in the area. These are black bears only — not brown bears, not grizzlies. They are not aggressive and have never historically been a nuisance or threat in this area. The main concern is trash/food left outside. As a precaution, bear spray is not a bad idea. Do not leave food or garbage outdoors.

Deer are plentiful and frequently spotted on the property — they blend beautifully into the surroundings. One of the real treats: deer often pass underneath the treehouse sauna deck.

Other wildlife on property: ants, bats, mice, raccoons, bobcats (we share the land with all of it).

**Mountain biking:** Yes — bring the bikes. The trails on the private property are well-maintained and great for all levels. Excellent for kids to explore and get into nature. The property's 25+ acres plus adjacent land gives real range to ride.

**No hunting on the property.**

**Firearms:** For any questions about firearms, contact Mike directly via the contact page. (Mike appeared on History Channel's Top Shot, Season 3.)
